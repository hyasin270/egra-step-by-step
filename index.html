ICT child test · v2 · recorded on real WhatsApp · 4 Oct 2026

The coach gives the test. The recording scores it.

Built for a coach who has never seen EGRA or EGMA. One child at a time; each step is one WhatsApp message with the card to use and the exact words to say; the coach records and never counts anything. One set of cards per grade for the whole term. Children are found by name, class and class teacher, never by roll number. Every screen below is real WhatsApp on the NIETE sandbox, with the operator's account set to English and five test children voiced from scripts.

Your questions

What each confusing line meant, and what replaced it

How does the coach find the children? One grade, or a mix?

One grade per visit (3 or 5). The list is grouped by classroom with the class teacher, in the roster's own words: "Grade 3 - A · Teacher: …". Two in three visits need one classroom. Each teacher can get only their own room's children on WhatsApp.

Drop roll numbers?

Gone from every message, the form and the portal. The roster has every child's full name, the section (92% of classes) and a class teacher (99%). Two classmates with the same name get the father's name when the roster has it.

Printed passages "rotated": which variant is in the child's hands?

No rotation. In EGRA every child in a round reads the same form. Each coach carries one set per grade for the whole term, colour-coded (Grade 3 green, Grade 5 blue), "Set A · Oct–Dec 2026" on every card. Next term's set comes next term.

A4 PDF to print? Is that how EGRA does it?

Yes: A4, laminated, one task per side; the child sees only the story. The print pack is ready: 6 cards and one coach page, 16 pages per coach.

"First sounds from the coach's copy"?

EGRA's "initial sound" task (the adult says a word, the child gives its first sound), mainly for Grades 1–2; and a 5-page paper sheet the bot never mentioned. Both gone.

Interstitials in Urdu though my language is English?

Your sandbox account had been switched to Urdu for the first demo recording and not switched back. Back to English; every screen below is in English. Only the words said to the child are in Urdu (and English for the English story).

"Give the maths strip to the other child", but this was my first child?

The old design had the next child write maths while the current child read. Removed. Maths is oral, one child at a time: no strip, no photo, no child number.

Who reads the sums? Two maths pages?

The child reads the pairs and sums on one maths card and says the answers; the coach points. The coach reads the two word problems aloud, as EGMA does; the words are in the message.

Why did the form ask Urdu questions the card didn't show?

Urdu EGRA asks questions (Pakistan's version 5; we ask 3). The coach asks them aloud after turning the card face down; they are never printed for the child. They are now in the step message itself.

"This child's key says 56". How does the coach count words per minute?

They don't. The "key" was the test child's answer sheet. The AI counts from the recording; on the May children it agreed with the enumerators more often than a second trained person did (Urdu 74% vs 58% within ±5 words).
